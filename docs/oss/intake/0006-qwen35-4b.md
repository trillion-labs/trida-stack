## Why we needed something here

Training a 4B base from scratch is not the research. The research is the two-stream
block-diffusion method, which needs a competent causal base to start from.

## Alternatives considered

| 후보 | 왜 선택하지 않았나 |
|---|---|
| A Llama-family base | the acceptable-use terms are a field-of-use restriction we cannot pass on from an Apache-2.0 repository — rejected at the licence gate, not on quality |
| Train a base from scratch | cost without research value |

## Score

| 기준 | 점수 | 근거 |
|---|---|---|
| A 기능 적합성 | 20 | the size, the tokenizer and the quality are what the method needs |
| B 라이선스 적합성 | 20 | Apache-2.0 on the weights, with **no restriction propagating to derived models** — this is the reason our own weights can be released openly |
| C 유지보수 활성도 | 16 | an actively released model family, but a base model is a fixed artefact once chosen |
| D 보안 대응 | 15 | weights carry the usual untrusted-checkpoint surface; we load only from the trusted repository |
| E 통합·이탈 비용 | 12 | changing the base means retraining. This is the highest switching cost in the stack after `torch`. |
| **총점** | **83** | |

## Decision

Adopt. The licence check on a **base model** is different in kind from a licence
check on a library: a restriction on the base propagates to every derived
checkpoint we publish, and no amount of our own work removes it. Apache-2.0 here is
what makes Trida2.0-4B releasable.

## Obligations we take on

Attribution of the base model in the model card and in
[`COMPLIANCE.md`](../../../COMPLIANCE.md). The derivation must be stated plainly
wherever the derived weights are published.

## Re-evaluation trigger

Annually, and necessarily whenever a new base is considered — at which point the
licence gate is the first question, before any benchmark.
