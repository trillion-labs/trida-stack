**Important distinction.** Upstream SGLang is Apache-2.0 and would pass cleanly. The
failure is inherited: we consume SGLang *through* the HybridDiffusion fork, whose
repository root and `eval/` serving stack are PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0
(see `inference/sglang/NOTICE`). The patched backend inherits the noncommercial
restriction from its base, not from SGLang.
